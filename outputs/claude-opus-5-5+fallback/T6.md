# Resolving mRNA → surface protein for SSTR1

Throughout: **[S]** = standard practice in the field, **[J]** = my judgement/design choice, **[OK]** = outside knowledge not contained in the packet.

---

## 1. Main conclusion / recommendation

Do **not** try to solve this with one assay. Build a **calibrated ladder**: a reference system that converts an arbitrary signal into receptors-per-cell, and then propagate that calibration into progressively messier sample types.

**Primary assay: quantitative flow cytometry (QFC) on intact, non-permeabilized cells, reported as Antibodies Bound per Cell (ABC) using a bead-based MESF/PE calibration curve** [S]. This is the only assay that simultaneously gives (i) a continuous number, (ii) per-cell resolution, (iii) surface-restricted measurement, and (iv) the ability to gate on tumor cells and exclude the stroma/immune contamination that the packet's own composition analysis showed is the dominant artifact in this disease.

**Confirmatory/therapy-relevant assay: saturation radioligand binding on intact cells at 4 °C, reported as B<sub>max</sub> (sites/cell) and K<sub>d</sub>** [S]. For a *ligand-based* therapy this is the number that actually matters — ligand-accessible, correctly folded binding sites — and it is antibody-independent, so it is orthogonal to the QFC failure modes.

**Anchor for both: an isogenic trio** — SSTR1-knockout, parental, and a doxycycline-titratable SSTR1 line with an extracellular epitope tag (N-terminal HiBiT or ALFA tag) [J][OK for the specific tags]. The KO defines background; the tag ladder defines the calibration curve and the limit of quantification.

**For archival FFPE: quantitative multiplex immunofluorescence with digital image analysis, normalized to cell-pellet calibrator cores of known receptors/cell embedded in every block/run** [J, this bridging design; the underlying mIF/digital quantification is [S]]. Report "receptor-equivalents per tumor cell," not an H-score.

**Expected effect size sets the bar.** The packet's ARSI effect is β = −0.34 log2, i.e. a **~27% difference in mRNA**. Even if that transmits 1:1 to protein, no assay with >25–30% inter-sample CV will see it at small n. This is the single most under-appreciated constraint in the whole design.

## 2. Evidence

### Direct evidence (from the packet)

- SSTR1 is **down in CRPC** (log2FC −1.18, FDR 0.047) and **down in ARSI-exposed mCRPC** (unadjusted p = 0.013; site-adjusted β = −0.34, p = 0.077, FDR 0.334 → **tier 2, not replicated at the stated threshold**).
- **composition_rho = 0.0146** — the cleanest value in the candidate table. Whatever SSTR1 is doing, it is not a stroma/mast-cell/ischemia artifact. This is the strongest single argument for spending bench money on it.
- **su2c_median_log2FPKM = 1.25** (≈2.4 FPKM): low-to-moderate, well above the floor-level genes (CHP2 = 0, SNTG2 = 0.09) but below DPP4 (2.33). This directly predicts a **low-density receptor**, which is why detection limit (§6.5) is the central technical risk.
- SSTR1 correlates with AR activity (rho 0.19, p = 0.002), and the effect is **concentrated in lymph node and bone biopsies** — this dictates both the in vitro perturbation (AR axis) and the tissue-handling plan (bone = decalcification problem).
- The packet states the effect is "consistent with my bench result" but shows no such data — so I treat the bench result as **unverified** in this design.

### Indirect evidence

- The two strongest replicating tier-1 hits, **TRPM8 and DPP4, are androgen-regulated**, and the packet explicitly concludes the most reproducible signal is loss of AR output. SSTR1's AR correlation places it in the same class. **Prediction:** ARSI treatment of an AR-positive prostate line should lower surface SSTR1 — this converts an association into a testable causal in vitro experiment with a clean "treated vs untreated" number.
- Hallmark "Protein Secretion" is down in CRPC (adj. p = 9.5e-07). ## 3. Reasoning

**Why QFC as primary.** The packet's headline finding is that 34% of downregulated hits were composition artifacts and 20 of the top 25 were non-tumor signal. Any bulk protein assay (western, lysate ELISA, bulk MS) reinherits exactly that flaw: a lower SSTR1 signal in a metastasis could be fewer receptors per tumor cell, or fewer tumor cells in the sample. **Flow cytometry is the only quantitative surface assay that measures per tumor cell by construction**, because you gate (EpCAM⁺/CD45⁻/CD31⁻, DAPI⁻ live singlets) before you read the SSTR1 channel [S]. That single property makes it the right answer for *this* dataset's specific pathology.

**Why radioligand binding as confirmation.** GPCR antibodies are the worst-validated antibody class [S][OK — this is a well-documented, field-wide problem]. If both an antibody assay and a ligand-binding assay, which share no failure modes, give the same rank order across samples, the number is real. And B<sub>max</sub> is literally the quantity a therapeutic ligand will see.

**Why the isogenic ladder is non-negotiable.** With five SSTR subtypes sharing substantial homology [OK], and with a low-abundance target, "specific staining" is an assertion until you show signal collapses to background in a KO. [J] I would refuse to interpret any patient-tissue number generated with an antibody that has not been run against an SSTR1-KO.

**Why not a presence/absence call.** The biology demands a continuous number: the hypothesis is a ~27% shift, not an on/off switch. Scoring "positive/negative" would discard the entire effect.

## 4. Assumptions

1. SSTR1 mRNA and SSTR1 surface protein are at least monotonically related. **This is the assumption the experiment is designed to test, not one it can rely on.**
2. The samples assayed are the right ones: comparison is **ARSI-exposed vs ARSI-naive mCRPC**, matching the packet's replication design — not CRPC vs localized, which the packet itself flags as confounded by castration + metastasis + chemo + autopsy-vs-surgery.
3. Available AR-positive prostate models express SSTR1 at a measurable level. If all standard lines are negative, the in vitro arm collapses and the whole program must start in PDX/organoid or tissue. **This must be checked first, cheaply, before any assay development.** [J]
4. Surface SSTR1 is not so rapidly cycling that fixation/handling artifactually changes the surface pool. (Controlled in §7.)
5. Enzalutamide/abiraterone exposure in vitro is an acceptable model of clinical ARSI exposure. (The packet already warns: **ARSI exposure ≠ ARSI resistance**.)

## 5. Alternative explanations (for a negative or discordant result)

- **mRNA down, surface protein unchanged** — post-transcriptional buffering, long receptor half-life, or reduced turnover. Entirely plausible and would kill the mRNA-based rationale while leaving the therapeutic hypothesis alive.
- **Total protein down, surface fraction up** — trafficking compensation; consistent with the "Protein Secretion down" pathway result. The therapy would still work.
- **Both down but the absolute number is too low to matter** — the most likely outcome in my estimation [J]. A statistically significant 27% drop from an already-therapeutically-insufficient baseline is biologically uninteresting for a ligand-based therapy. **Measure the absolute number before you care about the difference.**
- **The difference is driven by biopsy site, not treatment** — the packet already shows the effect is concentrated in LN and bone. Bone metastases differ in tumor content, decalcification history, and hypoxia.
- **Antibody cross-reactivity with SSTR2/3/5**, whose own regulation may differ, producing a spurious or diluted signal.

## 6. Proposed experiment / next step

### 6.1 Which assay, and what I rejected

**Chosen:**
- **(A) Quantitative flow cytometry, ABC/cell** — primary, all live-cell sample types. [S]
- **(B) Intact-cell saturation radioligand binding, B<sub>max</sub> sites/cell** — orthogonal confirmation, antibody-free, therapy-relevant. [S] [OK: SSTR1-preferring ligands such as the CH-275 class / L-797,591-type selective agonists exist, as does competition with subtype-selective blockers to carve SSTR1 out of pan-somatostatin binding; verify current availability.]
- **(C) Cell-surface biotinylation (membrane-impermeant NHS-biotin) → streptavidin capture → targeted MS (PRM) with heavy-labeled peptide standards**, fmol/µg surface protein. [S] Tier-2 orthogonal only — see rejection logic below.
- **(D) Quantitative multiplex IF with calibrator cell pellets** — the only option for archival FFPE. [J for calibrator bridging]

**Rejected, with reasons:**

| Alternative | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| **Western blot** | Cannot distinguish surface from intracellular; GPCRs run as heterogeneous glycosylated smears; densitometry has a narrow linear range; bulk lysate reinherits the packet's composition confound. Semi-quantitative at best. |
| **Standard chromogenic IHC + H-score** | Ordinal, reader-dependent, non-linear (enzymatic amplification), poor dynamic range at low abundance. Produces something close to a presence/absence call — explicitly excluded by the question. Keep only as a tumor-morphology/tumor-mask companion. |
| **RNAscope / qPCR / snRNA-seq** | Still mRNA. Does not address the gap. (Useful only as a concordance covariate.) |
| **Lysate sandwich ELISA** | Total, not surface; requires a validated non-overlapping antibody pair, which is unlikely to exist for SSTR1; same composition confound. |
| **Radioligand binding on membrane homogenates** | Standard and sensitive, but homogenization mixes plasma membrane with ER/Golgi/endosomal receptor — destroys the surface/internal distinction that is the point of the exercise. Use intact cells instead. |
| **Targeted MS as the *primary* assay** | A 7-transmembrane, low-abundance, glycosylated receptor yields few tractable proteotypic peptides; sensitivity for a ~2 FPKM transcript in a surface-enriched fraction is a genuine coin-flip. Excellent as orthogonal confirmation if it works; too risky to build the program on. [J] |
| **In vivo PET with a labeled ligand** | The ultimate integrative number (SUV<sub>max</sub>), but no SSTR1-selective clinical tracer exists to my knowledge; approved somatostatin tracers are SSTR2-preferring [OK]. Long-term goal, not the next experiment. |

### 6.2 What the assay actually reports

- **QFC** reports **ABC (antibodies bound per cell)** — a calibrated relative number in absolute-looking units. It equals receptors/cell **only if** the antibody is monovalent (use a 1:1 PE-conjugated Fab), is used at true saturation (verify with a titration plateau), and the fluorophore:protein ratio matches the bead standard [S]. Otherwise treat it as **receptor-equivalents/cell** — a cross-sample-comparable relative scale. I would report it as such and say so explicitly in any figure legend. [J]
- **Radioligand B<sub>max</sub>** reports **ligand-accessible binding sites per cell** (fmol/10⁶ cells → sites/cell via Avogadro). This is the closest thing to a true receptor count, and it is the number a therapeutic ligand "sees." It is **not** total receptor protein — misfolded or occluded receptor is invisible, which is a feature here.
- **PRM-MS** reports **fmol peptide per µg of streptavidin-captured surface protein** — a mass concentration, convertible to copies/cell only with a counted cell input and a known capture efficiency.
- **mIF on FFPE** reports **mean membranous fluorescence intensity per tumor cell**, which is arbitrary until bridged to the calibrator pellets; after bridging, **receptor-equivalents/cell**.

### 6.3 Establishing that the signal is SSTR1

This is the part I would spend the most effort on, in this order [S for the logic; [J] for insisting on all five]:

1. **CRISPR SSTR1-KO** in the positive model line. Signal in KO = the true background. An isotype control is *not* sufficient and I would not accept it. The validated metric is the **KO-subtracted** ABC.
2. **Dose-dependent recovery**: dox-titratable SSTR1 re-expression in the KO restores signal monotonically. Gives both specificity and the calibration curve.
3. **Family cross-reactivity panel**: HEK293 lines each overexpressing SSTR1, 2, 3, 4, 5 at matched levels (verify by a shared extracellular tag). The antibody must read SSTR1 ≫ others. Report the cross-reactivity ratio numerically.
4. **Two independent antibodies** against non-overlapping extracellular epitopes must rank samples concordantly (report Spearman rho across the sample set).
5. **Orthogonal modality agreement**: antibody ABC vs radioligand B<sub>max</sub> across ≥6 models spanning the dynamic range; report the regression slope and R².
6. **Ligand-side specificity**: radioligand binding must be competed by unlabeled SSTR1-preferring ligand and abolished in SSTR1-KO.

### 6.4 Surface vs intracellular

- **Non-permeabilized vs permeabilized staining on the same sample, same antibody, same session.** Report **surface ABC, total ABC, and the surface fraction (surface/total)** as three separate numbers. [S] The surface fraction is itself a biologically interesting readout given the "Protein Secretion down" pathway result.
- **Membrane-impermeant biotinylation at 4 °C** labels only surface protein; a parallel **permeant biotin** arm, and a **no-biotin** arm, bound the specificity. [S]
- **Acid wash / protease strip**: brief trypsin or low-pH wash should remove surface signal but not intracellular signal — a direct functional demonstration of which pool you are reading. [S]
- **Extracellular HiBiT knock-in + membrane-impermeant LgBiT** gives a surface-only luminescent readout with no fixation and extraordinary sensitivity; add permeabilization to get total. [OK for the specific technology; [J] for proposing it here.] This is my preferred solution in engineered models and the best route around the low-abundance problem.
- **Temperature control**: all live-cell staining at 4 °C in the presence of sodium azide to freeze trafficking. [S] Warm staining will internalize receptor and systematically under-read surface pool.
- **Confocal colocalization** with a plasma-membrane marker (e.g., Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase) as a qualitative sanity check only — not the quantitative readout.

### 6.5 The detection-limit problem

**What sensitivity is required.** Two constraints, and the second is the harder one:

1. **Absolute**: a 2.4-FPKM GPCR plausibly corresponds to a surface density in the **~10²–10⁴ receptors/cell** range [J, order-of-magnitude inference only — the mRNA→protein conversion factor is not measurable from this packet]. QFC with a good Fab and PE reaches roughly **~10³ ABC/cell** reliably, sometimes a few hundred [S/OK]. Intact-cell radioligand binding with 10⁶–10⁷ cells can resolve **a few hundred sites/cell** [OK]. So the target sits uncomfortably close to both floors.
2. **Differential**: to detect β = −0.34 log2 (~27%) you need a **limit of *quantification*, not detection**, with inter-sample CV ≤ 25–30%. With 30% CV, detecting a 27% difference at 80% power/α=0.05 needs roughly **25–30 samples per group** [S calculation]. That number should be stated up front to anyone funding this.

**Mandatory LOD/LOQ determination** [S]: run the dox-titratable ladder across ≥6 expression levels, each in ≥3 independent replicates; define LOD = KO-background mean + 3 SD, LOQ = the lowest level with CV ≤ 20%. Publish both numbers.

**If SSTR1 is near or below LOQ — what to do, in order of preference:**
1. **Increase input and integration**: more cells per tube (10⁶ events), longer radioligand counting times, pooled replicates. Cheapest first move.
2. **Switch to the ligand assay**, which has a better floor than antibody flow for low-density GPCRs. [J]
3. **Amplify without losing linearity**: tyramide signal amplification or proximity-ligation/rolling-circle readouts — but these **must be re-calibrated against the ladder**, because amplification is nonlinear and will compress or expand the scale. [S caution]
4. **Enrich before measuring**: surface biotinylation capture concentrates the surface pool ~10–100× before MS.
5. **Report an upper bound, never "negative."** If the sample reads below LOQ, the correct output is e.g. "≤ 600 receptors/cell (95% CI)" — still a number, still comparable, and still decision-useful.
6. **Make the honest call**: if surface SSTR1 in untreated mCRPC is, say, <1,000 sites/cell, **the ligand-therapy hypothesis is dead regardless of the ARSI effect**, and the program should stop. [J] This is the most valuable possible outcome of the experiment and should be pre-specified as a go/no-go.

| **Surface vs total** | Fully resolvable | Resolvable (±permeabilization) | **Not resolvable** — only membranous-vs-cytoplasmic *localization pattern* as a weak surrogate. State this as a hard limitation. |
| **Key new problem** | Model may not express SSTR1 at all | Enzymatic dissociation can cleave extracellular epitopes → run a **cold mechanical vs enzymatic dissociation comparison** and pick a non-cleaving enzyme [S]. Viability ≥70% or discard. | Fixation/epitope masking; **bone biopsies are decalcified**, which destroys many epitopes [S][OK] — and the packet says the SSTR1 effect is concentrated in bone and LN. This is a direct collision between where the signal is and where the assay works. |
| **Mitigation** | Screen 6–10 lines by QFC first; if all negative, engineer one and move the discovery work to PDX | Fresh is mandatory; cryopreserved cells need a freeze/thaw recovery factor measured on a reference line | Prefer **LN/soft-tissue** FFPE; for bone, restrict to EDTA-decalcified blocks and model decalcification as a covariate; record fixation time and block age, both of which degrade signal |
| **Perturbation available?** | Yes — enzalutamide ± R1881, the key causal test | Limited (PDX in vivo ARSI possible) | No — observational only |

**[J] Sequencing of the work:** do the in vitro causal experiment *first*. If enzalutamide does not lower surface SSTR1 in an AR-positive line where it lowers SSTR1 mRNA, the entire mRNA-based inference is falsified cheaply, before any patient tissue is consumed.

### 6.7 Replicate structure and normalization

**Replicates** [S]:
- *In vitro*: **biological replicate = independent passage, independently treated, stained on a separate day** (n ≥ 4; n = 5 if CV >25%). Technical duplicates within a run are *not* replicates and must not be used as n. Drug treatment ≥72 h; include a vehicle arm processed in parallel on the same day.
- *PDX/organoid*: n ≥ 3 independent models per arm, ≥3 animals/wells per model; model is the unit of replication, nested.
- *Patient samples*: **patient is the unit of replication, one lesion per patient** — explicitly mirroring the packet's SU2C design. Target **≥25–30 per arm** per §6.5. **Paired pre-/post-ARSI biopsies from the same patient, if obtainable even at n = 10–15, are worth more than 60 unpaired samples** because they remove inter-patient variance [J, strongly held].

**Normalization** [S unless noted]:
1. **Per cell** — the primary denominator. Count cells, don't infer them.
2. **Per cell-size**: GPCR density scales with membrane area; report ABC and ABC/FSC-A (or ABC per membrane-dye intensity) and check whether the conclusion survives both. [J]
3. **Surface-protein loading control for MS**: ATP1A1 / Na⁺-K⁺-ATPase as the denominator for the biotinylated fraction; report the intracellular-contamination marker (e.g., calnexin or GAPDH) as a QC number, not as a normalizer.
4. **Batch bridging** — the single most important normalization: **one frozen reference cell pellet (mid-range SSTR1) and one calibration bead lot run in every single batch**, in every sample type including the FFPE arm. All values are expressed relative to the bridging standard. Reserve enough of a single bead/pellet lot for the whole study. [J for the "single lot for the whole study" rule, but this is how it is done well.]
5. **Tumor-content normalization for tissue**: all per-tumor-cell values computed within a pan-cytokeratin⁺/AMACR⁺ tumor mask with a pathologist-verified region. **Report tumor content as a covariate and show the result is not explained by it** — this is the direct protein-level analogue of the packet's composition_rho filter, and it is the lesson the packet most insists on.
6. **Covariate adjustment in the patient arm**: model `surface_SSTR1 ~ ARSI_exposure + biopsy_site + tumor_content (+ decalcification for FFPE)`, matching the packet's `expression ~ ARSI + site` structure so the protein and mRNA analyses are directly comparable.
7. **Pre-specify thresholds and the primary endpoint before unblinding** — the packet sets thresholds in the first code cell before inspecting gene lists; the bench work should hold itself to the same standard. [J]

## 7. Key controls

**Specificity:** SSTR1-KO (the true background); isotype/Fab-only; unstained; secondary-only; SSTR2/3/4/5 overexpression panel; two non-overlapping antibodies; unlabeled-ligand competition.
**Quantification:** QuantiBrite-type bead ladder; dox-titratable expression ladder spanning the LOQ; frozen reference pellet in every batch.
**Compartment:** ±permeabilization; impermeant vs permeant biotin; no-biotin; acid/protease strip; 4 °C vs 37 °C staining.
**Biology/positive control:** an AR-regulated surface protein from the packet's own tier-1 list — **TRPM8 or DPP4 (both cell_surface = True)** — measured in the same samples. If enzalutamide lowers surface DPP4/TRPM8 as expected but not SSTR1, that is an internally controlled negative, not an assay failure. [J — this is the most valuable control in the design, and it comes straight out of the packet's table.]
**Handling:** dissociation-method comparison; viability gate; time-from-collection recorded and tested as a covariate (the packet's ischemia/immediate-early-gene concern has a protein analogue).
**Analytical:** blinded sample IDs through acquisition and image analysis; randomized run order; treated/untreated never processed in separate batches.

## 8. What would falsify the hypothesis?

The hypothesis under test: *ARSI exposure reduces cell-surface SSTR1 protein on prostate cancer cells, from a baseline high enough to support ligand-based therapy.*

It is falsified if:
1. **KO cells give the same signal as parental** → the antibody is invalid; every downstream number is meaningless. (Kills the assay, not the biology.)
2. **Enzalutamide reduces SSTR1 mRNA but not surface ABC/B<sub>max</sub>** in an AR-positive line, while the TRPM8/DPP4 positive controls do respond → surface SSTR1 is post-transcriptionally buffered; the mRNA-based inference does not transmit to protein.
3. **Baseline surface density in untreated mCRPC is at or below LOQ** (my pre-specified no-go: a prespecified minimum receptors/cell, set from the literature on what ligand-based therapies require [OK]) → the therapeutic premise fails irrespective of direction of change.
4. **Treated vs untreated difference has a 95% CI that includes zero and excludes a pre-specified minimum meaningful effect** (I would set this at ~1.3-fold, matching β = −0.34) → adequately powered null, not an underpowered null.
5. **The difference disappears after adjusting for tumor content and biopsy site** → it was composition again, exactly the failure mode the packet was built to catch.
6. **Surface fraction rises as total falls**, leaving surface density flat → trafficking compensation; the therapy target is intact.

## 9. Uncertainty / limitations

- **SSTR1 is tier 2 in the packet's own table** (su2c_FDR = 0.334). The pre-specification and the clean composition_rho justify follow-up, but this is a borderline signal being promoted on prior belief plus an unshown bench result. [S caution]
- **mRNA→protein copy-number inference is not possible from this packet.** My 10²–10⁴ receptors/cell estimate is an order-of-magnitude guess [J][OK] and the assay must be built to survive being wrong by 10×.
- **The effect size is small relative to typical protein-assay precision.** This is the dominant risk of a false-negative and the reason for the n ≥ 25/arm and paired-design emphasis.
- **GPCR antibody quality is the dominant risk of a false-positive.** If no antibody passes the KO test, the antibody-based arms (QFC in patient samples, mIF in FFPE) are simply unavailable, and the program is restricted to radioligand binding + engineered models — which cannot be applied to archival tissue at all.
- **Bone metastases are where the signal is and where the assays are weakest** (decalcification, low tumor content, difficult dissociation). This is a structural, not solvable, tension.
- **ARSI exposure ≠ ARSI resistance** (the packet's own caveat) carries over unchanged into the protein study; the treated/untreated contrast may not be the clinically relevant contrast.
- Single-timepoint surface measurement does not capture receptor turnover or recycling rate, which also affect ligand-therapy dosimetry [J].
- I have not seen the packet's referenced bench result; if it already contains surface-protein data, parts of this design are redundant.
